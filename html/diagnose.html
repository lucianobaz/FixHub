Diagnose

Start with the most common problems.

Use the list below to find a quick cause and a simple solution.

No power

Possible cause: the charger, cable, or outlet may be faulty.

Try: check the power cable, try another outlet, and look for lights on the device.

Slow performance

Possible cause: too many programs or low storage space.

Try: close unused apps, restart the computer, and remove old files.

Blue screen

Possible cause: a driver problem or a system error.

Try: restart the computer, update drivers, and check recent changes.

Internet problems

Possible cause: weak connection or router issues.

Try: reboot the router, check the cable, and reconnect to Wi-Fi.

No display or black screen

Possible cause: loose video cable, monitor set to the wrong input, or cable plugged into the motherboard instead of the graphics card.

Try: check the HDMI or DisplayPort cable, verify the monitor is powered on, and plug the cable directly into your graphics card.

Random restarts

Possible cause: processor overheating, a failing power supply, or a loose power cable.

Try: check that cooling fans are spinning, clean dust from vents, and make sure the power cable is firmly plugged in.

Overheating and loud fans

Possible cause: dust buildup inside fans and heatsinks, blocked airflow, or dried-out thermal paste.

Try: clean fans and filters with compressed air, place the PC in an open area, and avoid blocking air vents.

No audio or sound

Possible cause: sound is muted, wrong output device selected in Windows, or loose headphone/speaker jack.

Try: check system volume, click the speaker icon to pick the right output device, and reconnect audio cables.

No bootable device found

Possible cause: the computer cannot find the storage drive, or a connected USB drive is confusing the startup order.

Try: unplug any USB flash drives or external drives, restart the computer, and check if the drive appears in BIOS.

Computer beeps on startup

Possible cause: the motherboard detected a hardware problem during startup, most commonly loose RAM or graphics card.

Try: turn off the PC, unplug power, open the case, and firmly press the RAM sticks and graphics card into their slots.

USB ports or devices not working

Possible cause: power overload on USB ports, a damaged USB port, or disabled USB controllers.

Try: plug your mouse or keyboard into a port on the back of the PC, and test the device on another computer.

Computer freezes completely

Possible cause: too many demanding programs open at once, low available RAM, or storage drive reading errors.

Try: press Ctrl+Alt+Delete to close frozen apps, restart the computer, and remove programs you no longer use.
