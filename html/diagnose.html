Diagnose

Start with the most common problems.

Use the list below to find a quick cause and a simple solution.

No power

Possible cause: the charger, cable, or outlet may be faulty.

Try: check the power cable, try another outlet, and look for lights on the device.

Slow performance

Possible cause: too many programs or low storage space.

Try: close unused apps, restart the computer, and remove old files.

Blue screen

Possible cause: a driver problem or a system error.

Try: restart the computer, update drivers, and check recent changes.

Internet problems

Possible cause: weak connection or router issues.

Try: reboot the router, check the cable, and reconnect to Wi-Fi.
