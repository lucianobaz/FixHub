Maintenance

Prevent problems before they start.

Good habits can help your computer stay faster, cooler, and more reliable over time.

Clean hardware regularly

Dust buildup can cause overheating and reduce performance. Clean vents and fans carefully.

Keep software updated

Updates often fix security issues and improve stability. Install them when available.

Use antivirus protection

Protect your system from malware that may slow down your computer or cause strange errors.

Back up important files

A backup helps you recover data if a drive fails or the system crashes unexpectedly.
